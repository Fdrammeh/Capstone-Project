# Minneapolis Attractions

Minneapolis offers museums, parks, historic landmarks, performing arts, and family-friendly activities.

## Museums and Cultural Attractions

- **Minneapolis Institute of Art:** An art museum featuring collections from different cultures and historical periods. Check current exhibitions and visiting hours.
- **Walker Art Center:** A contemporary art museum known for modern and experimental art exhibitions.
- **Mill City Museum:** A museum exploring Minneapolis history and the city's flour-milling industry.
- **American Swedish Institute:** A cultural center featuring Nordic heritage, exhibitions, and historic architecture.
- **Minneapolis Sculpture Garden:** An outdoor art garden featuring sculptures, including the well-known Spoonbridge and Cherry.

## Landmarks and Outdoor Attractions

- **Stone Arch Bridge area:** A popular Mississippi Riverfront area with scenic views and walking routes. Check current bridge access before visiting.
- **Minnehaha Regional Park:** A large park featuring Minnehaha Falls, walking paths, and picnic areas. Waterfall conditions vary by season.
- **Chain of Lakes:** Includes Bde Maka Ska, Lake Harriet, and Lake of the Isles, with opportunities for walking, cycling, and seasonal recreation.
- **Guthrie Theater:** A performing arts venue known for theater productions and views of the Mississippi River from its public areas.

## Planning Your Visit

Outdoor attractions may have seasonal closures or weather-related restrictions. Museums, theaters, and cultural sites may require tickets or reservations. Verify current hours, admission prices, accessibility, and event schedules before traveling.
