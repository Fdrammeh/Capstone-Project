# Popular Chicago Attractions

## Millennium Park

Millennium Park is a popular downtown public park. Visitors can see Cloud Gate, often called "The Bean," enjoy public art, and attend seasonal events. The park is close to other downtown attractions.

## Art Institute of Chicago

The Art Institute of Chicago is a major art museum with collections spanning many periods and cultures. Visitors should check current exhibitions, admission details, and opening hours before visiting.

## Navy Pier

Navy Pier is a lakefront destination with restaurants, shops, entertainment, and seasonal attractions. It offers views of Lake Michigan and the Chicago skyline. Some activities require separate tickets.

## Field Museum

The Field Museum features natural history exhibits, including dinosaurs, ancient cultures, and science displays. It is located near the lakefront museum campus.

## Shedd Aquarium

Shedd Aquarium features aquatic animals and exhibits about freshwater and marine environments. Check ticket availability and daily hours before your visit.

## Willis Tower

Willis Tower is a well-known Chicago skyscraper. Its Skydeck offers elevated city views. Visitors should check ticket options and visibility conditions when planning a visit.

## Planning Tips

Group nearby attractions together to save travel time. Check official websites for current hours, ticket prices, special events, and accessibility information.
