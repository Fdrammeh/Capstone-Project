# Chicago Food and Activities

## Chicago Foods to Try

Chicago is known for deep-dish pizza, Chicago-style hot dogs, Italian beef sandwiches, and a diverse restaurant scene. Restaurants offer cuisines from many cultures and a wide range of price points.

## Deep-Dish Pizza

Deep-dish pizza has a thick crust and is typically baked in a deep pan with generous layers of cheese, toppings, and tomato sauce. It is a popular choice for visitors who want to try a Chicago specialty.

## Chicago-Style Hot Dogs

A traditional Chicago-style hot dog is often served on a poppy seed bun with mustard, relish, onions, tomato, a pickle spear, sport peppers, and celery salt. Traditional versions do not use ketchup.

## Free and Low-Cost Activities

Visitors can explore public parks, admire public art, walk along parts of the lakefront, and enjoy city architecture. Some museums offer special free-admission days, but availability and eligibility vary.

## Family Activities

Families may enjoy Navy Pier, the Shedd Aquarium, the Field Museum, and Millennium Park. Check age recommendations, ticket costs, and opening hours before visiting.

## Evening Activities

Chicago offers theater performances, live music, comedy shows, and restaurants. Availability depends on the date and venue, so advance reservations may be useful.

## Food Planning Tips

Check restaurant menus and prices before going. Ask about dietary needs and allergies directly with the restaurant. Make reservations for popular restaurants when possible.
