MILWAUKEE, WISCONSIN — TRANSPORTATION GUIDE

PUBLIC BUS SERVICE

The Milwaukee County Transit System operates local bus routes serving Milwaukee and parts of the surrounding county. Buses can connect visitors with downtown, shopping areas, neighborhoods, and selected attractions.

Travelers should check current routes, fares, operating hours, and service alerts before departure. Not every attraction is directly served by a single bus route.

INTERCITY TRAIN SERVICE

Amtrak serves Milwaukee through its intercity rail station. Train service can be useful for visitors traveling between Milwaukee and other cities, including Chicago. Schedules, ticket prices, and service availability should be confirmed with Amtrak before booking.

AIR TRAVEL

Milwaukee Mitchell International Airport serves the Milwaukee area. Travelers can use ground transportation options such as taxis, rideshare services, rental cars, or available public transit connections. Check current airport transportation details before arrival.

DRIVING AND PARKING

Driving can be convenient for visiting destinations across the Milwaukee area. Downtown and popular attractions may have paid parking, parking garages, or limited street parking. Review parking restrictions and fees before leaving a vehicle.

WALKING

Downtown and the Historic Third Ward have walkable areas with restaurants, shops, and attractions near one another. Walking distances between more distant neighborhoods may be considerable, so plan routes according to weather, time, and mobility needs.

BICYCLING

Bicycling can be an option for exploring parts of Milwaukee, including selected lakefront routes and trails. Check route conditions, bicycle availability, traffic rules, and seasonal conditions before riding.

TAXIS AND RIDESHARE

Taxis and rideshare services can provide direct transportation between hotels, attractions, restaurants, and the airport. Availability, wait times, and prices vary.

TRANSPORTATION PLANNING TIPS

1. Plan the route before leaving your hotel.
2. Check current public transit schedules and service alerts.
3. Compare driving, public transit, walking, and rideshare options.
4. Allow extra travel time during major events and busy periods.
5. Confirm current fares, parking fees, and ticket requirements.

Transportation schedules and prices change. Consult official transit, rail, and airport sources for current information.
