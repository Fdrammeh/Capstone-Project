# Transportation in Chicago

## Chicago Transit Authority

The Chicago Transit Authority, known as the CTA, operates city buses and the 'L' train system. These services connect many neighborhoods, downtown destinations, and some areas near major attractions.

## The 'L' Train

Chicago's elevated and subway trains are commonly called the 'L'. Different lines serve different parts of the city. Visitors should check routes, service alerts, and station accessibility before traveling.

## Buses

CTA buses serve many streets and neighborhoods that are not directly beside train stations. Check route maps and arrival information when planning a journey.

## Airport Transportation

O'Hare International Airport and Midway International Airport have public transportation connections to the city. The best route depends on the airport, destination, luggage, time of day, and current service conditions.

## Walking

Walking can be convenient in downtown areas such as the Loop and the Magnificent Mile. Wear comfortable shoes and allow extra time when traveling between attractions.

## Taxis and Rideshare

Taxis and rideshare services provide direct transportation but can cost more than public transit. Travel time and cost vary with traffic and demand.

## Transportation Tips

Check current fares, schedules, and service alerts before traveling. Allow extra time during rush hour and major events. Keep belongings secure on crowded trains and buses, and confirm the route before boarding.
