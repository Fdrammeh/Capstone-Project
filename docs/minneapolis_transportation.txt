# Minneapolis Transportation Guide

Minneapolis has public transportation, cycling routes, pedestrian paths, and regional connections to the wider Twin Cities area.

## Public Transportation

- **Metro Transit buses:** Local buses connect downtown Minneapolis with neighborhoods, shopping areas, and other destinations.
- **METRO light rail:** Light rail lines serve parts of Minneapolis and connect the city with destinations in the Twin Cities, including Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport and the Mall of America on the Blue Line.
- **Northstar and regional services:** Regional transit options may serve selected destinations. Check current routes and schedules before traveling.

## Airport Transportation

Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport (MSP) serves the Minneapolis and Saint Paul area. Travelers can use available light rail, buses, taxis, rideshare services, rental cars, and airport shuttles, depending on their destination.

Confirm the current route, terminal, schedule, and fare before leaving the airport.

## Walking and Cycling

Minneapolis has many sidewalks, park paths, and bicycle routes. The Chain of Lakes and Mississippi Riverfront are popular areas for walking and cycling.

Seasonal weather can affect trail conditions and bicycle access. Check local conditions before setting out.

## Driving and Parking

Rental cars and personal vehicles can be useful for destinations outside central Minneapolis. Downtown parking availability and prices vary by location and event.

## Transportation Tips

Check Metro Transit's official website or app for current routes, service alerts, schedules, and fares. Transit schedules and services can change, especially during construction or severe weather.
